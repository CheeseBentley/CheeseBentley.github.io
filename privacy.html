Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Purpose

This application is used privately with rclone to access and download files from Google Drive.

Data Access

The application requests read-only access to Google Drive files and metadata.

Data Usage and Storage

Google Drive data is accessed for personal file management and downloaded to local storage. OAuth credentials are stored locally by rclone.

Data Sharing

This application does not intentionally transmit Google Drive file contents to third-party analytics or advertising services.

Access Revocation

Google account access can be revoked through Google Account's third-party connections settings.
